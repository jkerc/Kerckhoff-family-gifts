How to use this app

Logging in

Pick your name and enter the family password. The app opens your own list automatically.

The gift table

Each person's gifts appear as a compact table. Click any column header to sort. Hover a row to reveal action buttons.

Claiming gifts

When viewing someone else's list, check the box in the first column to claim a gift or idea. The recipient never sees claims on their own list.

Gift ideas

Click "Add idea" when viewing someone else's list to suggest a gift. Ideas are hidden from the recipient but visible to everyone else. Anyone can edit or claim an idea.

Archiving

Click the box icon on any row to archive a gift after it's been given. Archived rows are hidden by default — click "Show archive" at the bottom of the table to reveal them.

Copying data

Because the gift list is a real HTML table, you can select rows and paste directly into Excel or Google Sheets.

Sync

The colored bar at the top shows sync status. Green = all changes saved to the cloud and visible to all family members.
