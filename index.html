How to use this app

Logging in

Pick your name from the dropdown and enter the family password. The app opens your own wishlist automatically.

Your wishlist

Your name card is selected when you log in. Use "Add to my list" to add gifts you'd like. Anyone can edit any gift — use the pencil icon to update details, add a link, or leave a comment.

Claiming gifts

Click another family member's name to see their wishlist. Check the box next to a gift to claim it — no duplicates. The recipient can't see who claimed their gifts.

Adding gift ideas for others

When viewing someone else's list, click "Add gift idea" to suggest a gift. It's visible to all other family members but hidden from the recipient. Anyone can edit an idea to add a link or notes, and anyone can claim an idea just like a regular wishlist item — check the box to let others know you're buying it.

Archiving gifts

After a gift has been given, click the archive icon (box) to move it to the archive. Archived gifts are hidden from the main list but preserved for history. Toggle "Show archive" to view them.

Real-time sync

The green bar at the top means changes sync instantly across every family member's device.

Overview tab

See everyone's wishlist progress at a glance — total wishes, gifts claimed, and a progress bar per person.
