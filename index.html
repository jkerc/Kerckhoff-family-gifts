How to use this app

Logging in

Everyone in the family uses the same password. The default is family123. Once in, select your name from "Viewing as" in the top right so the app knows who you are.

Your wishlist

Click your own name card to open your wishlist. Use "Add to my list" to add gifts you'd like to receive, including name, occasion, price, and a link. Edit or delete with the pencil and trash icons.

Claiming gifts

Click another family member's name, then check the box next to a gift to claim it. This prevents duplicates. The recipient cannot see who claimed their gifts.

Adding gift ideas for others

When viewing someone else's list, click "Add gift idea" to suggest a gift. It appears in a separate section marked with your name. They can dismiss ideas they don't want.

Real-time sync

Click the cloud icon in the top bar to connect Firebase. Once connected, all changes sync instantly across every family member's device. A green status bar confirms you're synced.

Managing family members

Click "Add member" to add someone. Hover over a card and click the X to remove them — this also removes all their wishlist items.

Overview tab

See everyone's wishlist progress at a glance — total wishes, gifts claimed, and a progress bar per person.

Connect Firebase sync

1
Go to console.firebase.google.com and create a free project.
2
In your project, open Build → Realtime Database, click "Create database", choose a region, and start in test mode (you can lock it down later).
3
Go to Project Settings (gear icon) → Your apps → click the web icon </> to register a web app. Copy the firebaseConfig object shown.
4
Paste the {...} config object below and click Connect.
